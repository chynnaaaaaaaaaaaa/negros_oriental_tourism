About the Province

Welcome to
Negros Oriental

Nestled in the Visayas region of the Philippines, Negros Oriental is a province of extraordinary contrasts — from the teeming coral gardens of Apo Island to the mist-cloaked peaks of Mount Talinis. Its capital, Dumaguete City, is lovingly called the "City of Gentle People."

Be Prepared

Essential Travel Tips

Everything you need before you pack your bags.

All travel tips
Best Time to Visit

The dry season from November to May offers the best weather. December to February is coolest and most comfortable.

How to Get There

Fly into Dumaguete-Sibulan Airport from Manila, Cebu, or take a ferry from Cebu Pier 1 or Bohol.

Budget & Currency

Very budget-friendly. Most attractions cost under ₱200. Typical daily budget: ₱1,500–3,000.
